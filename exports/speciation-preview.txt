::paragraph dropCap::
The Cambrian explosion produced roughly 20 animal phyla in about 20 million years — and no new phyla have appeared in the half-billion years since. That is the biological benchmark for rapid speciation. If you want to understand how absurd the AI analog looks against it, you need to sit with that number for a moment.

::paragraph::
Now look at what's happened in AI since 2022. Frontier model variants are proliferating at a rate measured in weeks. The AI Flash Report summarizes it as plainly as possible: *on average, a new AI model arrives every three days.*

::section id="section-01" num="01" pillar="Pillar 1 — Capital Markets" title="The sedimentary layer: where the nutrients came from" shortLabel="Capital"::
::paragraph::
In the biological Cambrian, the triggering event was oxygenation. In the AI Cambrian, the equivalent is capital. Not metaphorically. Literally: the infrastructure buildout that enabled frontier-scale training required a level of capital deployment that didn't exist for any prior technology cycle.

::paragraph::
In 2025, AI captured roughly 52.5% of all global venture capital — the first time any technology category has claimed majority of VC in a single year. OpenAI and Anthropic together represented about 14% of all worldwide VC dollars in 2025.
